data/korean.txt is the Korean word frequency list ko_50k.txt (2018) of FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), generated from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus
(http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php; P. Lison and J. Tiedemann, 2016, OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large
Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles, LREC 2016).
